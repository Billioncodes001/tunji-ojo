## The February meeting
On 14 February 2025, the Federal Ministry of Information published an account of Tunji-Ojo receiving the leadership of the Nigeria Union of Journalists, FCT Council. The delegation was led by its chair, Grace Ike.

## The commitment described
Tunji-Ojo said journalists were welcome to engage with his office and acknowledged the media’s role in holding public officials accountable. The official account records the meeting and the remarks made; it is not an independent assessment of media access across the ministry.

## From access to useful reporting
For this independent journal, a strong article does more than repeat a statement. It identifies when something happened, links to supporting material and makes clear which conclusions are established and which require more evidence.

That distinction matters when reporting on services that affect large numbers of people. An announcement can open a line of inquiry; a later article can examine implementation and ask what changed.

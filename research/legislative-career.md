## 10. Contradictions and flags

1. Ambulances: 10/12/14 — use "over ten".
2. NDDC probe amount: N40bn (allegation), N81.5bn (Jan–May 2020 spending) — different things; do not conflate.
3. Wikipedia's "appointed Minister of Interior on 16 August 2023" is imprecise — Interior assigned 20 Aug.
4. 2023 election: 25 Feb (poll) vs 26 Feb (declaration).
5. Screening: questioned then bowed — do not claim uneventful.

## 7. Bottom line

Safe to publish (HIGH): born 1 May 1982, Akoko North-West, Ondo State; only child of the late Tunji Ojo and Mrs Yinka Tunji-Ojo; father killed in Kaduna in 1992; primary schooling in Oyin Akoko, Kaduna and Akure; FUTA Staff Secondary School senior prefect 1998; OAU 1999–2002 then London Met (ECE 2005, MSc Digital Communication & Networking 2006); Certified Ethical Hacker 2006; CEO of Matrix IT Solutions at 24; consulted for World Bank, PTDF, JAMB, NFVCB, NCDMB, NHIS, NSIA; co-founded New Planet Project Ltd (2009); honorary doctorate JABU 22 Jan 2021; married to Abimbola (née Otegbeye) with two children; Christian.

Do not publish: "Adaobi," "Wilberforce Group," "NICE Projects," any earned PhD, CIPM/ACFE/NCS membership, a "BTO Foundation," the "BSc International Relations" or "double degree in ICT" claims, or a marriage year.
